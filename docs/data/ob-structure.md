# CNA order-of-battle structure and counter-roster sources

Mechanics-only source inventory for SPI's *The Campaign for North Africa*. Unit names, hierarchy, numeric ratings and chart references are extracted; rulebook prose and counter artwork are not reproduced. The companion [ob-sample.md](ob-sample.md) begins the named-counter enumeration with the Operation Compass-era forces.

## Organization: historical names versus game counters

The OA is organized by nationality and **formation/grouping sheets**, not by a complete army → corps → division command tree. The indentation joins division HQs, brigade/regimental HQs and their subordinate counters. Independent armor, infantry, gun, anti-tank, anti-air, engineer and recce units have separate groupings. Historical sizes in names are not necessarily game equivalents: many British tank “regiments” are battalion-equivalents. Sources: [§4.45–4.46, n9](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n9.jpg), [Commonwealth OA key, n115](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n115.jpg), [Italian OA key, n146](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n146.jpg), [German OA key, n161](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n161.jpg).

## The full roster and the 1,800-counter count

**The complete physical-counter roster is clearly enumerable in the scan, but it is not a numbered “Order of Battle Appendix” and is not 1,800 named combat units.** The unnumbered countersheet-reproduction appendix at the end of the Air/Logistics rules, printed pp40–44, starts at [n90](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n90.jpg). It reproduces counter sections **1–9**, **200 pieces per section**, for **1,800 physical counters**. Section 9's reverse is also reproduced: it is not another 200 pieces. The count includes combat/HQ units, aircraft/SGSU, transport/replacement/supply counters, prisoners and other status markers. The OCR also states the same 9 × 200 inventory in §4.6. The accompanying OA charts are the named land-unit roster, but they do not state a single total number of land counters.

### Missing OA joins and scan inconsistencies

The 61 Sirte, 62 Marmarica and 63 Cirene counters are visible on [n90](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n90.jpg), and their scenario/arrival availability is supplied by [n77](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n77.jpg) and [n144](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n144.jpg). Their individual OA sheets are not present in the scanned n146–160 sequence. The sample therefore begins those counter identities but leaves their absent ID/TOE/morale/characteristics `?`. It does not duplicate Sabratha's profile as a guessed template.

The sample separately flags displaced OCR, Central India Horse's conflicting ID note, French Marine company deployment versus OA arrival, and Guards/armored-brigade arrival disagreements. These are source limitations, not numeric cells repaired by historical inference.
