# Air and naval numeric data

Primary source: SPI, *The Campaign for North Africa*, archived rulebook/chart scans. Values below were read from the linked images and cross-checked against the [complete OCR](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/The%20Campaign%20for%20North%20Africa_djvu.txt). OCR is corrupted for several rotated charts; image values take precedence. `—` denotes a printed dash, not an unreadable cell. Dice ranges for two dice are sequential readings (11–66, with each digit 1–6).

## Air-to-air combat

Sources: [n105, §§45.4–45.5](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n105.jpg); [OCR, §45.36](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/The%20Campaign%20for%20North%20Africa_djvu.txt).

### Bomber formation bonus — §45.36

| Bombers on mission | TacAir bonus per bomber |
|---|---:|
| 1–5 | 0 |
| 6–17 | +1 |
| 18 or more | +2 |

The zero entry expresses the absence of a bonus below the published 6-plane threshold. Dive bombers are ineligible (§45 procedure). Transports receive no formation bonus (§34.53); formation benefits do not apply to nonfighters on night missions (§39.4).

### Maneuver adjustment — §45.4

| Absolute maneuver-rating difference | Adjustment magnitude |
|---|---:|
| 0 | 0 |
| 1–3 | 1 |
| 4–7 | 2 |
| 8–14 | 3 |
| 15–20 | 4 |
| 21 or more | 5 |

The higher numerical Maneuver Rating is better. Under the rules' explicit calculation and worked example (§45 procedure), add the adjustment to the higher-rated plane's TacAir differential and subtract it from the lower-rated plane's differential. The chart caption's wording about adding/subtracting a plane's TacAir rating is less explicit than the rule calculation.

## Port capacity and efficiency — §55.3

Source: [n109, chart page 15](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n109.jpg). Personnel/unit throughput is represented by the printed **Stacking Points In/Out** columns, not a head-count column. Tonnage and stacking limits apply per Operations Stage; maximum tonnage includes shipping in and/or out.

| Port | Efficiency level | Stacking points in | Stacking points out | Maximum tonnage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Tripoli | 10 | 10 | 15 | 15,000 |
| Bizerta* | 10 | 10 | 15 | 3,333 |
| Alexandria | 10 | 5 | 10 | 15,000 |
| Tobruk† | 5 | 1 | 3 | 1,700 |
| Benghazi | 3 | 2 | 5 | 2,500 |
| Mersa Matruh | 1 | 1 | 2 | 250 |
| Bardia | 1 | 0‡ | 1 | 400 |
| Sollum | 1 | 0‡ | 1 | 250 |
| Derna | 1 | n/a | 1 | 300 |
| All others | 1 | n/a | n/a | 100 |

| Footnote / numerical qualification | Value |
|---|---|
| * Bizerta: first possible Axis activation roll | Game-Turn 35 |
| † Tobruk: listed undamaged efficiency | 5; initial efficiency is below 5 owing to harbor blockage, but this chart does not specify its initial numeric value |
| ‡ Bardia/Sollum: zero-stacking unit exception | Any 1 unit of 0 Stacking Points may enter |
| Capacity loss per efficiency level lost | 1 / listed efficiency level |

Reinforcements are exempt from port capacity/current efficiency limits. Replacement Points arriving by Axis convoy use stacking throughput, not maximum supply tonnage. Stacking-point and tonnage throughput limits are independent.

## Legibility and source discrepancies

No requested numeric cell is unreadable. The OCR garbles many chart cells, so image transcription is authoritative. The supplied TacAir +3/+6 examples were corrected above. The monthly shipping example and formation bonus are supplied by rules OCR rather than by the six specified chart images.
