# Operation Compass-era named-counter enumeration

Mechanics-only extraction from SPI's *The Campaign for North Africa*. This begins the full named land-counter roster; it is not a list of 1,800 combat units. See [ob-structure.md](ob-structure.md) for the physical-counter manifest and the OA/chart joins.

## Scope and reading the numbers

The scenario anchor is §60.23, the Italian Campaign, covering Game-Turns 1–20 and including Operation Compass; initial deployments are §§60.31 and 60.41: [scan n77](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n77.jpg). The British/Commonwealth side is described here as the Western Desert Force / later Eighth Army side, not as an invented Eighth Army HQ counter. Tables cover the maneuver formations, named support/reserve units, Italian coastal/oasis garrisons and theater arrivals transcribed below. A theater arrival does not assert participation in a Compass battle. This is a concrete sample, not a complete scenario initialization: aircraft, SGSU, supply/transport markers, replacement points and all unrelated later formations are outside these tables.

- One row is one named counter. Historical `Regt` or `Brigade` in a battalion-sized unit name does not create additional counters. Parent is the OA assignment; attachments are recorded in the notes rather than duplicating a counter under two formations.
- `TOE` is the published arrival strength: numeric weapon-component counts or the numeric maximum when the OA prints `N`; `U@` strength is transcribed as its actual number. `—` is a printed not-applicable/unrated entry; `?` is unreadable or not supplied by the available source. A dash is not a zero.
- Offensive and defensive assault, barrage, anti-armor, AA and armor protection are **per TOE-point characteristics**, not a synthesized whole-counter combat strength. When a counter has several weapons, semicolon-separated numeric cells follow the **same component order** as the weapon column. No averaging is performed. Parentheses are the chart's restricted ratings, not uncertainty.
- `CPA profile` is the printed unit-ID allowance. `CPA weapon` is the separate weapon allowance; do not silently replace one with the other. The source describes assigned weapon CPA / lowest-component CPA and the fixed-CPA `*` exception (§4.46, [n9](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n9.jpg)). `+` is the published motorization annotation. Fuel is the **weapon fuel-rate column**; it is not an initial fuel stock and is not a standalone infantry/HQ fuel demand. Missing nonweapon fuel is `?`.
- `Morale basic` and `Morale arrival` remain separate. `—` in arrival morale means no separate parenthesized arrival rating is printed, not morale zero. Group headers publish the common morale; individual overrides appear in their rows.
- `D` means initially deployed. Arrival fractions are `Operation Stage/Game-Turn` as printed in the OA chart, not calendar dates. Source disagreements are retained below.

The tables below begin the enumeration with **409 named counter rows**. No claim is made that all 409 are simultaneously on-map or that they are the complete 1,800-piece manifest.
