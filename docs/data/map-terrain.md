# CNA map terrain: Vassal-image transcription

## Source and scope

Source: [CNAv2.1.0.vmod](https://obj.vassalengine.org/gls/9/4/CNAv2.1.0.vmod), ZIP members `images/CNA Map Vassal Mitch Guthrie 2021.png` (14310 × 4632 pixels) and `buildFile.xml`. The module was downloaded and those members extracted. The combined image was cropped into the five named sheet zones and enlarged for reading. The [original sheet A Terrain Key](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n187.jpg) supplied the symbol distinctions; the [original sheet B scan](https://archive.org/download/campaign-for-north-africa/page/n188.jpg) also supplied coordinate-label anchors. No rulebook text or map artwork is reproduced here.

This supersedes the earlier, much smaller original-scan transcription. It is **a conservative, pixel-assisted transcription, not a complete playable map database**. Source fill colors and distinctive patterns were matched within hex interiors, with visual comparison of enlarged crops against the terrain key. Partial rough, desert, salt-marsh, and mountain patches were recorded where the symbol was distinct. Ridge bands were not automatically treated as mountain terrain. Uncertain mixtures, small boundary patches, obscured symbols, and uncertain coastal readings remain `?`; they were not filled from geography or adjacent hexes.

The Vassal rendition includes module-specific edits, so this file describes that rendition rather than asserting exact equivalence to the unmodified 1979 board. Transport, settlements, rivers, wadis, ridges, escarpments, and other overlays are not encoded in the one-character base-terrain grids. A clear hex can still contain those features.

## Coordinates and footprint

Each line is a coordinate row `rr`; each character after the separating space is successive column `cc`, starting at `01`. Thus character 3 on row `30` of A identifies **A0330**. Row labels and spaces are not cells. The display follows printed coordinate order, not north-up image order: columns increase toward the top of the combined image and rows increase toward the right. The stagger is retained through the coordinate mapping, not through added spaces in the grid.

Centers were derived from the module's sideways HexGrid origins, spacings, numbering offsets, and stagger settings. The named zone polygons determine sheet membership; their bounding rectangles determine the image crops. See the upstream [HexGridNumbering implementation](https://github.com/vassalengine/vassal/blob/master/vassal-app/src/main/java/VASSAL/build/module/map/boardPicker/board/mapgrid/HexGridNumbering.java) for the numbering transformation.

The earlier B envelope ending at column 57 omitted northern columns: the original B scan visibly labels columns 58–61. C's envelope here includes column 51 from the Vassal zone-center mapping. These dimensions replace the previous transcription's envelopes; they do not establish a rectangular board.

**Do not interpret every `?` as a playable hex.** The rectangular display includes positions outside the irregular zone polygons. Ungridded blue sea, holding boxes, titles, and tracks are not manufactured into sea hexes. The last coordinate row falls outside the A–D zone-center footprints in the combined rendition and is preserved as unresolved padding, not inferred from a neighboring sheet. Original-sheet overlap/partial-edge hexes are not reconstructed. Malta is a separate zone and is not merged into A.
