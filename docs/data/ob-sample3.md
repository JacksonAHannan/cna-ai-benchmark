# Remaining CNA counters and inventory reconciliation

Continuation of [ob-sample.md](ob-sample.md) and [ob-sample2.md](ob-sample2.md). Mechanics only; the previous land roster is not repeated. One row represents one counter. A named SGSU is an air-support counter, **not an aircraft or a land battalion**. Aircraft counters have class labels but no individual squadron identity: the numbered occurrence labels below are transcription locators, not names printed by SPI.

`?` means a characteristic is unreadable or not supplied on the cited face. The SGSU number is transcribed as the **printed bottom number**, without inventing assault, TOE, aircraft type, strength, morale, or fuel. Aircraft silhouettes are not sufficient evidence for a specific aircraft model. Air-to-air, bombing and range characteristics therefore remain `?`; the reproduction does not print those ratings on these faces.
